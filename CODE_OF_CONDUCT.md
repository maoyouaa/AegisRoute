# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behaviour includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, and responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, sexualised language or attention, personal attacks, trolling, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through the security contact described in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and take proportionate corrective action.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
